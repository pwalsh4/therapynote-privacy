Privacy Policy

Effective October 7, 2026

In short: TherapyNote is a Chrome extension that helps invited physical therapists draft visit notes. Visit audio is transcribed and never stored. Transcripts stay on the clinician's computer. Text needed to draft a note is sent to OpenAI with identifiers removed and the patient's name and dates replaced by placeholders. We do not sell data, show ads, track browsing, or let anyone train AI models on this information.

1. Who we are and who TherapyNote is for

TherapyNote is developed and operated by Patrick Walsh, PT ("we", "us"). It is an invite-only tool for licensed clinicians at practices we work with; it cannot be used without an invitation. Patients do not use TherapyNote directly.

When a clinician uses TherapyNote, patient information is handled on behalf of the clinician's practice, which is a HIPAA covered entity. We act as that practice's service provider (business associate), and the practice remains responsible for its patients' records.

2. Information we handle

About clinicians

  • Google sign-in: your Google account email address, name and account ID, used only to confirm you are an invited clinician. We request only the basic sign-in scopes (openid, email, profile) and never see your Google password.
  • Practice: the name of the practice (clinic) your account belongs to, used to organise accounts and billing and to hold that practice's encryption recovery key (section 6). Belonging to a practice does not give anyone access to your patient information.
  • Usage records: which feature was used, the AI model, amounts of text processed, minutes of audio transcribed, and cost — used for operating and billing the service. These records contain no patient information.
  • Issue reports you choose to send from Settings, emailed to us with optional technical details (extension version, browser, screen, connection status).

About patients (protected health information)

Only what the clinician records or enters while documenting a visit:

  • Visit audio, recorded only after the clinician confirms the patient agreed to the recording.
  • The transcript, the clinician's therapist notes, and the text of an intake form the clinician chooses to attach.
  • Drafted notes, the patient's name as shown in the electronic health record, the visit date, case names, treatment goals, measurements, and reference articles saved to a case.
  • Text already in the note fields of the open PracticePro page, read so the draft can build on it.

The extension reads web pages only on PracticePro (ptpracticepro.com), and only to identify the patient on the open chart and to read and fill note fields. It does not read or record any other browsing.

3. How the information is used

Solely to provide TherapyNote's features: transcribing the visit, drafting and checking the note, filling it into PracticePro, keeping a clinician's notes available on their own computers, and providing support. We do not sell or rent information, use it for advertising, build profiles, or use analytics or tracking tools.

5. Service providers

  • Amazon Web Services — hosting, encrypted storage and email, under a business associate agreement.
  • OpenAI — drafting, checking and answering questions about notes, under a business associate agreement. Requests are sent with storage switched off, and under OpenAI's API terms this data is not used to train its models.
  • Deepgram — speech transcription, under a business associate agreement. Every request opts out of Deepgram's model-improvement program.
  • Google — sign-in only. No patient information is sent to Google.
  • Physiopedia — receives only short condition terms from the browser, never patient information.

6. How information is protected

  • All connections are encrypted (HTTPS/TLS), and stored data is encrypted at rest.
  • Before text leaves the clinician's computer, email addresses, phone numbers, Social Security numbers, record and account numbers and street addresses are removed. The patient's name and calendar dates are replaced with placeholders that are filled back in on the computer, so the AI provider never receives them.
  • Optional end-to-end encryption. A clinician can turn this on in Settings. Their notes, cases and templates are then encrypted on their own computer (AES-256) before they are uploaded, with a key that is created on that computer and never sent to us. Our server, and anyone with access to it, sees only encrypted data plus record IDs and timestamps. The clinician's other computers get the key by approval from a computer already set up, or with 12 recovery words that only the clinician holds.
    • Practice recovery key: each practice can create its own recovery key, held offline by that practice's leadership. Our server keeps only its public half. If a clinician loses every computer and their recovery words, the practice and we together can restore that clinician's access; neither can do it alone, and one practice's key cannot unlock another practice's clinicians.
    • If the key, the recovery words and the practice recovery key are all lost, the encrypted data cannot be recovered by anyone, including us.
    • End-to-end encryption protects stored data. To draft a note, the cleaned text described below still passes through our server to OpenAI; it is processed in memory and not stored.
  • Access is invite-only. Sign-in sessions expire after 24 hours, and a removed clinician loses access within about a minute. Each clinician's data is kept separate from every other clinician's.
  • Access to patient records on our server is logged by record ID, without note content. Server logs do not contain note text.
  • Signing out, or a different clinician signing in on the same browser profile, removes patient information stored by the extension on that computer.

Text cleaning reduces what is shared but is not full de-identification: clinical details, and names of other people mentioned during the visit, can remain in text sent for drafting, under the agreements above.

7. Retention and deletion

  • PracticePro is the record; TherapyNote keeps working copies, and removes them automatically on a schedule each clinician sets in the extension:
    • transcripts, therapist notes and intake text: 7, 14 (default) or 30 days after the visit's note is filed;
    • drafted notes and documentation grades: 30, 90, 180 (default) or 365 days after filing, or kept;
    • everything for a patient with no visits for 90, 180 (default) or 365 days, or kept.
    A note counts as filed once it has been filled into PracticePro or copied out. Visits whose note has not been filed are never removed automatically, so unfinished work is never lost.
  • Removal applies on all of the clinician's computers and in our cloud copy. Clinicians can also delete any visit at any time, and signing out removes patient information from that computer.
  • Each cleanup run is recorded in a log on the clinician's computer that contains counts only — no names, dates of service or content.
  • Database backups allow recovery for up to 35 days, after which deleted data is no longer recoverable.
  • To close an account and delete its data, email us at the address below. We confirm the request with the practice before deleting clinical records it may need to keep.

8. Patients

TherapyNote records a visit only after the clinician confirms the patient agreed. Patients who want to see, correct or ask about their records should contact their therapy provider, which controls those records; we will help the provider respond.

9. Google user data

TherapyNote's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google account information is used only to sign clinicians in, is never transferred for advertising or other purposes, and is never used to train AI models.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date. Significant changes will be shown in the extension.
