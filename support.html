Support

Help for clinicians using TherapyNote

Report a problem: in the extension, open Settings → Report an issue. It reaches us directly, and replies come to your account email. Or email patrick@progressionptequipment.com.

Please never include patient names or details. Describe what happened, not who it happened to.

Getting started

  1. TherapyNote is invite-only. Ask your practice administrator or email us to be added, using the Google account you will sign in with.
  2. Install the extension from the link you were given, then click the TherapyNote icon to open the side panel.
  3. Sign in with Google. A short walkthrough explains the workflow; it is always available in Settings → How to use TherapyNote.

Before you use it with patients

  • Turn on full-disk encryption (FileVault on Mac, BitLocker on Windows) and a screen lock.
  • Use your own Chrome profile, and sign out on shared computers.
  • Follow your practice's policy for patient consent to recording. TherapyNote asks you to confirm consent before the first recording for each case.
  • Review every drafted note before you sign it. AI can make mistakes.

Common questions

"Your email is not on the approved list"

Your Google account has not been invited yet, or you signed in with a different account. Contact us with the account email to use.

PracticePro shows as not connected

Open the patient's note page in PracticePro in the same window, then press Reconnect. You can still record and draft without it and copy the note.

A note box was not filled

Press Remap this box next to it and click the matching box on the PracticePro page.

Deleting your data

Old data is also removed automatically on the schedule you choose in Settings → Data retention (visits whose note hasn't been filed are never removed). Delete individual visits or cases in the extension at any time. To close your account and delete its data, email us from your account address. See the privacy policy for details.

Security concerns

If you believe patient information has been exposed, email us right away with "Security" in the subject. We respond as a priority and notify affected practices as our agreements require.
